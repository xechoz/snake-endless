# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Split the arcade Endless mode out of `xechoz/snake` into its own game,
  **Bloomtail: Endless** / **花尾蛇：无尽**.
- Keep the garden fruit art, skins, super fruit, feast and ad flow.
- Give the game its own manifest (`snake-endless`), save key and release build;
  campaign saves and progression are intentionally not migrated.
- Remove the campaign map, levels, stars, golden seeds and level difficulty
  settings from this repository.
